Privacy Policy

Last updated November 10, 2026

The short version: your timetable never leaves your device, and there is no account. PingPro does not collect your name, email, timetable, photos or any other personal information.

Mac app: no internet access and no ads. iPhone & iPad app: shows one small banner ad at the bottom, provided by Google AdMob. Those ads are non-personalised, and that is the only part of PingPro that uses the internet.

What PingPro stores

PingPro keeps your timetable — subjects, teachers, rooms and period times, whether the built-in example or one you scan or import — in a local file on your own device. On iPhone and iPad it is also shared with the PingPro Home Screen widget through an on-device shared container. It is not uploaded, backed up by us, or shared with the developer or anyone else.

Scanning a timetable

When you scan a screenshot or PDF of your timetable, the text is read on your device using Apple's built-in text recognition. The image or PDF you pick is never uploaded anywhere. PingPro only reads the specific photo or file you choose.

Importing and exporting

When you import or export a CSV, PingPro uses the standard system file picker and only reads or writes the exact file you choose.

Notifications

PingPro schedules local notifications (for example, "5 minutes before your next class") using Apple's on-device notification system. Nothing about them is sent to a server, because PingPro doesn't have one.

Advertising (iPhone and iPad app only)

The iPhone and iPad app displays a small banner ad using Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. This lets us keep PingPro free. The Mac app contains no ads.

  • Because PingPro is used by students, ads are requested with child-directed treatment turned on and a general-audience content rating only. This means ads are not personalised.
  • PingPro does not ask for permission to track you and does not use the advertising identifier to track you across other apps and websites.
  • To load and show an ad, and to measure and protect against invalid traffic, Google's ad SDK may collect certain technical information from your device, such as your device or advertising identifier, approximate location (derived from your IP address), ad interactions such as impressions and taps, and crash and performance diagnostics. We do not link this information to your identity, and we never give Google your timetable.
  • Google processes this data under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. More on how Google uses data from partner apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Data sharing

We do not sell your data, and we do not share your timetable or any personal information with anyone. The only third party that receives technical information is Google, through the ad banner described above, in the iPhone and iPad app.

Children's privacy

PingPro is designed for students of any age. It does not ask for or collect personal information from children, there is no account, and ads shown in the iPhone and iPad app are non-personalised and limited to general audiences.

Changes to this policy

If PingPro's data practices change, this page will be updated before the change takes effect.
