What it does

Built around the actual school day.

Not a generic calendar app — PingPro understands periods, breaks, and the two-week timetable rotation your school already runs on.

Notified before the bell

A quiet alert five minutes before every period ends — class or break — telling you exactly what's coming next.

Handles Week 1 / Week 2

Set it once and PingPro tracks the rotation itself — no relabeling your schedule every other Monday.

Any timetable, via CSV

Import your own school's schedule as a spreadsheet — periods, teachers, rooms, both weeks. Export it back any time.

Or type it in by hand

No spreadsheet? A built-in editor lets you fill in your timetable row by row, right inside the app.

Always running

Launches at login and lives quietly in the menu bar — no dock icon, no window to leave open.

Questions

Before you install.

Is the Mac app really free?

Yes — every feature, no trial period, no locked "pro" tier. The tip option in the pricing section above is optional and doesn't unlock anything extra.

Is there an iOS app?

Yes — free on iPhone and iPad, with a Home Screen widget for Now/Next at a glance. It's currently finishing App Store review; the link above will go live the moment it's approved.

Does this work with my school's timetable, not just one specific school?

Yes — the built-in schedule is just a starting example. Import your own via CSV (Week, Day, Period, Start, End, Subject, Teacher, Room) and PingPro runs entirely on your data.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. Your timetable lives on your device and every notification is scheduled locally — nothing is sent anywhere.

What if macOS says it's from an "unidentified developer"?

Shouldn't happen via the Mac App Store — Apple handles installation and verification automatically.
